Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This page explains how the Dr M Devendramma Books website handles information when you browse the catalogue, send an enquiry or place an order through WhatsApp.

1. About this website

Dr M Devendramma Books is an author bookstore. Customers can browse the public book catalogue without creating an account. The website does not provide an online payment gateway or complete a purchase directly; book orders and enquiries are handled through WhatsApp.

2. Information handled by the website

Bookstore content. The public website reads book details, author settings and visible gallery photographs from Firebase Firestore so that the catalogue and author information can be displayed.

Shopping cart. The browser stores only book record IDs and quantities in session storage so the cart can remain available during the current browser tab session. The website does not save your name, delivery location or alternate phone number in this cart storage.

Orders and enquiries. When you choose to order or send an enquiry, the website uses the details you enter to prepare a message and open WhatsApp. The website code does not write those customer details or the order to Firestore. The message is sent only when you choose to send it in WhatsApp. After you leave this website, WhatsApp and its service providers handle the message under their own terms and privacy information.

Administrator access. Google sign-in is used for the separate author administration area. Public customers do not need to sign in. The approved administrator's authentication is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication.

3. Hosting and third-party services

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Firebase/Google services to retrieve bookstore content. It also loads fonts and page styling resources from Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS CDN.

The website may link to WhatsApp for orders and enquiries and to Amazon India when an Amazon book link is provided for a book. These services are operated by third parties. When your browser loads or opens a third-party service, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, and may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Their own privacy notices and terms apply to your use of those services.

We do not control the privacy practices, availability, content or security of external websites and services.

4. Payments

No card, bank, UPI or other payment details are collected by this website. There is no payment gateway on the storefront. Any payment or purchase arranged directly with the author or through an external retailer is handled separately from this website.

5. Your choices

You can browse without creating an account. You can remove items from your cart or clear it using the cart controls. You do not have to submit an enquiry or send a WhatsApp message. You can also use your browser settings to manage session storage and external content.

6. Security and updates

Reasonable care is taken when maintaining the website and its connected services. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. This policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

7. Contact

For a privacy question about this bookstore website, please use the WhatsApp or phone contact details displayed on the Contact section of the main website.
