How this prototype applies the offers:
10% OFF = 10% of eligible subtotal. Buy 1 Get 1 = when a qualifying book is in cart, the cheapest second book priced within the configured free-book limit is discounted. Buy Any 2, Get 1 Free = with 3 or more book units, the cheapest additional unit is free up to the higher price of the first two units.
